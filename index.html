A Message From Your Colleagues 💌

Dear Nova Mae ,

As you move on to a new chapter, we just want to take a moment to say thank you.

Thank you for being part of our team and for sharing so many ordinary days that somehow became memorable. From busy workdays and stressful moments to random conversations, jokes, laughter, and everything in between, those little moments became part of our team's story.

Work isn't always easy, but having good people around makes the difficult days lighter. Your presence was one of those things that made the workplace feel a little more familiar and a little more enjoyable.

"Good coworkers become good memories, and good memories stay with us long after the work is finished."

We are genuinely happy for you as you begin this new journey. We hope the next chapter brings you new opportunities, new experiences, and plenty of reasons to be proud of yourself.

Wherever you go next, we hope you meet people who appreciate your efforts, support your dreams, and make you feel that you're exactly where you're supposed to be.

And even though you won't be working beside us anymore, the memories we've made together will always be part of the team.

So this isn't really a goodbye to the memories. It's simply a "see you around" and a wish that life treats you kindly wherever you go.

Thank you for being part of our team, Nova Mae. You will definitely be missed.
